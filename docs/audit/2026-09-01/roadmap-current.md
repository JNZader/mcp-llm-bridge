## Reading the board

Presence, proof, and delivery are separate questions:

1. **Implementation presence** — the source, test, or artifact exists in the inspected tree/history; it is not a closure claim.
2. **Verification currency/scope** — evidence identifies what was actually exercised, where, and what it excludes. Historical evidence is not silently renewed.
3. **Delivery state** — a local commit, an uncommitted current-tree change, an OpenSpec archive, and a published remote artifact are different states. Remote publication was not inspected.
4. **Acceptance/next action** — the exact missing contract or proof that can move the row forward.

## Evidence legend

- `[executed]` — command, exit code, and working context are recorded; this board ran only read-only state and structural checks.
- `[read]` — claim is read from a cited file and line range.
- `[inferred]` — conclusion derived from named read/executed anchors.
- `[assumed]` — not verified; the required proof is stated instead of guessed.
